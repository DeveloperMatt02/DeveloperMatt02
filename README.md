### About

I'm a Master's student in **Computer Science and Engineering – Artificial Intelligence** at Politecnico di Milano, after a **110/110** Bachelor's in Computer Engineering at Tor Vergata (Rome).

Alongside my studies I've spent five years as an **IT consultant**, running a small firm's entire IT environment: a Windows Server 2022 machine, Synology and QNAP NAS units with RAID-mirrored backups, user accounts and permissions, and a WireGuard VPN with NAT connecting three offices into a single private network.
